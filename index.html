Why a skunk works

Speed comes from focus.

The name comes from a tradition of small, autonomous engineering teams given a hard problem and room to solve it. We borrow the idea for AI: fewer meetings, a clear question, a build that can be tested.

01

Pick a real problem

Start from a question someone actually needs answered.

02

Build the smallest thing

The quickest system that can be tried and broken.

03

Test it against reality

Evaluate with data, failure cases, and honest limits.

04

Share the result

Publish what worked, what did not, and why.

Who it is for

Bring a problem, a skill, or both.

Students gain build experience on live problems. Faculty and partners get a fast, low-risk way to find out whether an AI idea is worth more investment.
