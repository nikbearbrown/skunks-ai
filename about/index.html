About AI Skunk Works

AI Skunk Works is a prototyping group at Northeastern University that turns AI questions into working systems.

Our mission

Find out quickly whether an AI idea works.

We keep teams small and cycles short. Each project starts with a specific question, produces something that can be run, and ends with an honest account of the results, including the failures.

The work is open to students, faculty, and outside partners who want to learn by building.

Prototype

Working software over proposals.

Evaluate

Tests, failure cases, and stated limits.

Teach

Every build is a chance to learn in public.

Partner

Northeastern talent applied to real challenges.
